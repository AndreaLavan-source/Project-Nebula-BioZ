# Project Nebula: Executive Summary & Investor Briefing

## 🎯 The Opportunity: The Vulnerability of Modern Biometrics
Static biometric authentication mechanisms—such as capacitive fingerprint readers, optical face recognition (FaceID), and static iris scanners—share a catastrophic system-level vulnerability: **they read dead surface architecture**. Because they map static geometry, they are fundamentally vulnerable to artificial presentation attacks, high-resolution lifting, deepfakes, and synthetic clones. Once a traditional biometric credential is leaked or bypassed, it is permanently compromised.

## ⚡ The Solution: Project Nebula
Project Nebula introduces a **128-Node Symmetrical Alternating Current (AC) Matrix** that redefines biometric cryptography. Instead of taking a static surface image, Project Nebula functions like a high-resolution **sub-surface biological camera**. 

By injecting precise, safe, sub-sensory multi-frequency AC waveforms through the palm, the system evaluates the complex electrical impedance network of deep human tissue. It tracks two distinct, interconnected physiological vectors:
*   🔴 **The Autonomic Activation Layer (Resistance Tracking):** Maps localized path conductance and active sudomotor sweat-duct alignment, capturing real-time autonomic nervous system activity.
*   🔵 **The Cellular Structure Layer (Capacitance Tracking):** Measures the precise phase-delay shifts caused by intact cellular lipid bilayers acting as microscopic dielectric insulators.

The result is a **living, dynamic cryptographic key** rooted in the body's natural bilateral structural asymmetry. Because synthetic films, silicone duplicates, or lifted prints lack living cellular membranes, they exhibit a phase shift of exactly 0°—making Project Nebula **inherently immune to spoofing, replication, or bypass.**

## 📊 Empirical Benchtop Validation
The foundational physics and mathematical signal processing loops have been successfully validated using a medical-grade **Analog Devices EVAL-AD5940BIOZ** front-end. Operating under an active hardware safety loop strictly clamped to a safe threshold ($\le$ 10 $\mu$A), technical testing confirmed:
1.  **Pristine Structural Baseline Stability:** A dry baseline tracking envelope ranging reliably between 340 $\Omega$ and 400 $\Omega$.
2.  **Precise Real-Time Correction:** Successful edge-computing execution of a C++ Quadrant Inversion Filter to eliminate systemic 180-degree hardware latency delays.
3.  **Deep Cellular Membrane Isolation:** Capturing repeatable, localized negative phase-delay angles of **-151.461°** at 100 kHz frequency sweeps, proving the ability to map deep, living tissue structures without hardware drift.
